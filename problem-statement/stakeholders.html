Stakeholder Requirements

Key Stakeholders

1. Business Stakeholders

Marketing Team

  • Needs:

    • Identify customers for targeted retention campaigns

    • Understand key factors driving churn

    • Segment customers by churn risk

  • Requirements:

    • Risk scores for each customer

    • Top churn predictors

    • Campaign effectiveness metrics

Product Team

  • Needs:

    • Understand which product features correlate with retention

    • Identify product improvements to reduce churn

  • Requirements:

    • Feature importance analysis

    • Product usage patterns of churners vs. retained

Finance Team

  • Needs:

    • ROI projections from retention efforts

    • Cost-benefit analysis

  • Requirements:

    • Predicted revenue saved

    • Model maintenance costs

2. Technical Stakeholders

Data Science Team

  • Needs:

    • Reproducible model training

    • Experiment tracking

    • Model versioning

  • Requirements:

    • MLflow integration

    • Feature store access

    • Model registry

DevOps/MLOps Team

  • Needs:

    • Automated CI/CD pipelines

    • Model monitoring and alerting

    • Scalable deployment

  • Requirements:

    • Kubernetes deployment manifests

    • Monitoring dashboards

    • Automated retraining triggers

3. Executive Stakeholders

C-Suite

  • Needs:

    • High-level dashboards

    • ROI reporting

    • Strategic insights

  • Requirements:

    • Executive summary reports

    • KPI tracking

    • Trend analysis
